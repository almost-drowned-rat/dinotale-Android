RetroArch slang shaders shipped with DinoTale (Settings > Graphics > Shader)

Taken from libretro/slang-shaders, commit 9230b1f24a6d0f28febe61711c0b255c4926b231
(https://github.com/libretro/slang-shaders), each with its own licence (also in the file's header):

crt/crt-easymode                 CRT Shader by EasyMode - GPL
scanlines/scanline-fract         by hunterk - public domain
handheld/lcd3x                   by Gigaherz - public domain
edge-smoothing/xbr               Hyllian's xBR-lv2 (xbr-lv2-standalone.slang) - MIT, Copyright (C) 2011-2022 Hyllian
edge-smoothing/xbrz              xBRZ freescale - MIT (Hyllian's xBR vertex code, Copyright (C) 2011/2016 Hyllian)

edge-smoothing/hqx               HQ2x by Lior Halphon, ported to slang by hunterk - MIT, Copyright (c) 2015-2016 Lior Halphon

Written for DinoTale (GPL-3.0-or-later): stock.slang (a pass-through pass) and edge-smoothing/xbr/xbr-lv2.slangp
(a one-pass preset for the standalone xBR-lv2 shader). The other .slangp files are libretro's presets as they are.

More presets: put any RetroArch slang preset (with the files it needs) into the "shaders" folder and select it as
"Custom" - see README.md.
